<h3><a href= "https://www.insper.edu.br/pt/docentes/guilherme-da-franca-couto-fernandes-de-almeida" target="_blank"> Guilherme ALMEIDA</a></h3>

I am an Assistant Professor of Law at <a href= "https://www.insper.edu.br/" target="_blank">Insper</a> currently visiting the Moral Science Lab with a grant from the São Paulo Research Foundation (FAPESP). I received both my PhD (2020) and my MsC (2017) in Law from the Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro. I also have an undergraduate degree in Law from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (2014). Before getting my current position at Insper (2022), I was a lecturer in Cognitive Science at Yale University (2021-2022), a researcher and lecturer at Fundação Getulio Vargas - Rio de Janeiro Law School (2018-2021), and a lecturer in Jurisprudence at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (2018-2019).

My research focuses on leveraging the tools of experimental cognitive science to answer questions about legal philosophy, such as when rules are violated and when statutes count as laws. Right now, I'm exploring how the different cases one encounters when dealing with a given problem pushes one towards vague standards (e.g., "drive carefully") or precise rules (e.g., "don't drive over 60 km/h").

Apart from academia, I love spending time with my wife and daughter and I am currently training for a marathon.
